Milis Arlain is the 4th son of a vassal of the Tubaths, holders of Lyafen manor
in Dobraine Hundred. He was fostered to the Tubaths at an early age after his
father disappeared on a mission along Noron’s Way. His clanhead, a distant
relation of his missing father, is still distraught over the father’s
disappearance. Milis is armored as a Poor Knight, with an average stipend. He
keeps a hunting dog named "Betsy"—the source of much annoyance in the knights’
quarters.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 84 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Dagger 75, Lance 96, Broadsword
  (Sword) 75, Axe 80, Shield 96.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 39 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 70, Oratory
  24, Unarmed 60, Foraging 56.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Backbag, small, canvas as
  the catalogue's Bag, sm, canvas; Leather Gauntlets as the catalogue's Leather
  Mantle; Knight Shield as the catalogue's Knight's Shield.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
