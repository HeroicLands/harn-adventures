Ervaen Banadar is the 2nd son of a vassal of the Blaundar, holders of Kylelaw
manor in Chyle Hundred. His parents are devout Laranians and would like nothing
more than for Ervaen to join the Lady of Paladins. His father leads his clan.

rich monthly stipend.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 81 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Dagger 70, Lance 90, Broadsword
  (Sword) 85, Axe 75, Shield 84.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 39 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 75, Oratory
  28, Unarmed 52, Foraging 52.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Backbag, small, canvas as
  the catalogue's Bag, sm, canvas; Comb, wood as the catalogue's Grooming Kit,
  common; Flint & steel as the catalogue's Tinderbox; Spoon, wood as the
  catalogue's Spoon, pewter; Tankard, wood as the catalogue's Tankard, pewter;
  Wineskin, small as the catalogue's Waterskin, 2qt.; Leather Gauntlets as the
  catalogue's Leather Mantle; Knight Shield as the catalogue's Knight's Shield.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
