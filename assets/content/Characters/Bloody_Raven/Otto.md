## Biography

Otto is a product of the seedier aspects of the Thardic Republic. His parents
were both Lia-Kavair and lay worshippers of Naveh in Shiran. Otto was trained as
an assassin by the Naveh and made three kills at the order of the temple. If
circumstances had been different, Otto may have been inducted into the
priesthood, but he refused kill the victim of his fourth assignment. As a test
of Otto's dedication to Naveh the temple had named his sister as his fourth
victim, he refused. Otto was sentenced to the Herth-Akan and survived, killing
all seven of his pursuers. After this, he chose to leave Tharda, but still waits
for the day the temple decides to send others to kill him. Otto still possesses
two vials of Gethepa poison.

Since leaving Tharda, Otto has made his way as a mercenary, spending three years
with a company in Rethem before moving on. He can be encountered almost anywhere
in Hârn, but prefers to stay away from Tharda, especially Shiran. Since leaving
the mercenary company, Otto has predominantly worked as a bodyguard to wealthy
merchants.

He still enjoys "the Hunt" as he calls it, and if circumstances allow, will
practice his stealth and stalking – only on one of these occasions has he
actually taken the life of his target. Otto is considering his next move, with a
goal of joining the ranks of the wealthy.

## Adventure Hooks

Stalked: For whatever reason, Otto has chosen to practice "the Hunt" on one of
the PCs. He will leave evidence that they could have been killed, but were left
alive.

Bodyguard: Otto is working as a bodyguard for a wealthy mercantyler the PCs have
dealings with.

Investigation: In a major city, PCs are hired to investigate sightings of
someone creeping around at night. Or, the PCs encounter Otto while they
themselves are creeping around at night.

The Grand Plan: Disguising his identity, Otto hires the PCs to break into the
residence of his employer. This is a distraction so that Otto can remove
valuables for himself, and kill his employer, blaming it on the PCs. He will
then take over the business himself.

Assassin: Someone has discovered Otto's talents and made him a very lucrative
offer. Either one of the PCs can be the target, or someone that they work for or
are otherwise associated with.

Blackmail: Otto has been using blackmail against a wealthy noble, or merchant.
PCs are hired to put a stop to the blackmailer, and recover the payments.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Mental Conflict (HM3 psionic, ML 47) is carried as a copy of the Spirit skill
  at that mastery; SoHL has no psionic talents.
- Melee 78 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Club 90, Dagger 84, Taburi
  (Dagger) 84, Shield 59, Round (Shield) 78, Spear 91, Sword 60, Shortsword
  (Sword) 80.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 46 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 82, Oratory
  55, Unarmed 84, Foraging 78.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Leather Gauntlets as the
  catalogue's Leather Mantle; Coudes, kurbul as the catalogue's Kûrbúl Coudes;
  Vial, glass as the catalogue's Vial, glass, medium.
- HM3 gear with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Taburi (thrown), Spear
  (thrown).
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
