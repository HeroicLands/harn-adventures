## Biography

The middle child of three daughters, Wynda was born to a gentle, but landless
family. Her father was steward to a minor manor lord, her mother a
lady-in-waiting. Raised in a good home, Wynda is energetic, practical, and
utterly pragmatic. Her parents tried hard to find her a husband, but her
unflinching propensity to bluntly speak her mind and sarcastic wit quickly drove
them away.

By the time she was 23, Wynda's parents were starting to worry about finding her
a husband. Fate intervened. While attending the Royal Chelebin Tournament in
Olokand, Wynda met Haselun [FFF 312-2], the son of the Standing Bear Inn's
proprietor. He was tall and gangly, and two years younger than her. Although he
was an odd, somewhat eccentric character she was still strangely drawn to him.
The feeling was mutual. Haselun was enthralled by this petite, acerbic, ball of
fire. In a whirlwind romance lasting less than a tenday, he courted her at every
possible moment. At the end of the tournament, he asked her father for Wynda's
hand in marriage. The son of a wealthy innkeeper was better than her parents had
hoped for; her father approved. The ceremony occurred two months later at the
Laranian temple in Tashal.

The couple has two children, son Celed (16) and daughter Danisa (15). The
passion between the two seems to have dimmed over the years and their
relationship is stormy. Haselun often uses faux-romantic terms for her like "the
aklash," "toxic midget," or "you rancorous fured old sow" (usually behind her
back). The terms don't faze her in the least. In truth, Haselun is intimidated
and terrified of her. Wynda is able to cow him at any time, usually with a
short, sharp cry of "Haselun!"

Although not a member of the innkeepers' guild, she is often a far more
effective innkeeper than her husband. She makes sure Haselun gets certain jobs
done or stays out of the way when she is handling difficult guests. When Haselun
is on the verge of meltdown, it is Wynda who steps in to clear up the mess. Her
manor upbringing stands her in good stead when dealing with the nobility.

## Adventure Hooks

The Mistress Of The House. The PCs arrive at the Standing Bear Inn. Wynda
welcomes them and assigns them to the Bull Room. As they head up the stairs,
they hear Haselun complaining that they are "low-born scum." He tries to get
them to leave. She tells them to stay put. Much chaos ensues.

Messengers. Wynda has letters and gifts to send to her children working in inns
elsewhere (location at the GM's discretion). When she hears the PCs are heading
that way, she offers a generous fee to take them. Haselun is desperate to know
what is in the letters and parcels.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 33 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Dagger 33.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 53 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 55, Oratory
  32, Unarmed 40.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Beltpouch,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Belt, leather as the
  catalogue's Belt, Waist.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
