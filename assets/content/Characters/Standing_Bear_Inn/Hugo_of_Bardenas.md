## Biography

Hugo the brewmaster is originally from Aleath in Kanday and is the uncle of Urso
of Bardenas [Aleath B15]. A master innkeeper himself, Hugo's true passion is
brewing ale. It is a skill he learned from his father, who travelled extensively
and brought back several new techniques.

Inheriting his wanderlust from his father, Hugo travelled extensively in his
youth. He lived in Shorkyne, Palithane and Trierzon for a couple years each,
leaving him impressively multi-lingual. He is currently learning Jarinese from
Caelan. His travels eventually brought him to Gardiren where he met Jonil of
Sarion.

One of the reasons for Hugo's wanderlust is that he is secretly homosexual. He
initially left Aleath when the rumors got too persistent. Hugo fell in love with
Jonil (50). The two men have been lovers for more than 30 years and Jonil is the
reason Hugo settled in Kaldor. They share a bed and have a black dog named
Niksin. Shaped a bit like a barrel, Niksin is very gentle, lovable and a
favorite of the staff and customers alike. Niksin has the run of the place and
always finds the sunniest spot. The men are discrete about their relationship.
Despite being middle-aged, officially, Jonil is just another of Hugo's
journeymen.

Hugo runs the brewery attached to the Standing Bear Inn in Olokand. Although
technically bonded to Haselun's [FFF 312-2] franchise, Hugo runs the brewery
almost as a separate establishment. He knows exactly what is needed to prepare
for the tournament. He orders his own materials and starts brewing when he is
ready to and not a moment before. The brewery is known far and wide for "Hugo's
Special Ale." It has a mighty kick, distinctive and effervescence. Only Hugo and
Jonil know the secret: double fermenting with honey and sweet herbs.

Hugo is well liked in the community. His pleasant demeanor, quiet and polite
nature, and quick wit with a joke have earned him many friends. These same
characteristics and his intimidating strength have made him completely and
utterly immune to Haselun's ranting and raving. Hugo and Wynda [FFF 399-3] get
along famously. It is their combined abilities that thwart Haselun's most
idiotic ideas.

## Adventure Hooks

The Gentle Giant. A massive man, Hugo is well known throughout the town. The PCs
have got into a scuffle with some of the locals. Hugo steps in, ending it with
his sheer presence. He invites the PCs back to the inn.

Find That Recipe. A rival brewer desperately wants to know Hugo's secret recipe,
so much so that he hires the PCs to infiltrate the inn and brewery and find out
the brewer's method. Only Hugo and Jonil know the technique and they are very
careful about hiding it.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 56 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Club 58, Stick (Club) 76, Dagger 33.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 74 as
  Discourse; Woodcraft 57 as Woodworking.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 78, Oratory
  29, Unarmed 54.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Beltpouch,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Belt, leather as the
  catalogue's Belt, Waist; Stick (2ft) as the catalogue's Stick.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
