## Biography

The ninth of 13 children, Esyra life has always revolved around her immediate
and extended family. She has siblings, cousins, and more, all across
Meselyneshire. When an obscure village is mentioned, she will often pipe up from
the kitchen, "Oh, my cousin lives there." These numbers have only grown since
she married Melgar. He comes from a family of 14 children. Together, they have a
modest nine children ranging from age 16 down to 5. Melgar is a teamster and
regularly away for work. Her eldest daughter Briele watches the little ones, but
they often come to see their mom at the Standing Bear Inn (in Olokand, Kaldor)
where she works.

Esyra's parents were servants at Cleriel Manor. She started learned to cook from
her mother, eventually finding employment in a guildsman's household in Olokand.
Wynda [FFF 399-3] went for dinner there and was delighted with the meal. She
promptly poached Esyra as the new head cook for the inn. Esyra is perfectly
happy with the rank of journeyman. Wynda makes sure she well paid.

A devout Peonian, Esyra spends holy days assisting at the temple across Mycepris
Square. She is a lay assistant in the church and leader of the choir. Esyra
loves to sing while she works. She leads the 'kitchen crew' in a dizzying array
of songs. Their musical prowess is well known (and appreciated) by the patrons
of the inn.

The combination of a vast extended family, her work at the Peonian temple,
meeting travelers at the inn, and being an extremely popular and gregarious
extrovert, mean that Esyra is one of the most connected people in the shire. She
is the person to talk to if you want to know what is going on. For her favorite
people, she is known for bringing out the 'good baking' to get them talking.
While Haselun grumbles when he sees her sitting and talking to guests, he
doesn't dare say anything. The food has never been better and the kitchen is
always in tiptop shape. Wynda has made it clear; they can't afford to lose her.
Well, that, and the last time he tried to chastise her, Esyra chased him out the
front door with a cleaver!

## Adventure Hooks

Children Underfoot. The PCs are visiting the inn. There are numerous children
coming in and out of the kitchen. One of the young boys, Rohn, comes up to them
and says, "My mom says you are here to [insert secret mission]." It appears
their mission is not so secret after a

Wine And Dine. The PCs want to impress a potential contact. They arrange for an
extravagant meal in one of the Standing Bear Inn's private dining rooms. Esyra
outdoes herself. However, she personally oversees the serving staff and lingers
a bit too much trying to overhear their business. Is she a spy? If so, for whom?

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 58 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Dagger 52, Cleaver (Axe) 64.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 67 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 45, Oratory
  26, Unarmed 32.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Cleaver as the
  catalogue's Handaxe; Beltpouch, leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch,
  leather, sm; Belt, leather as the catalogue's Belt, Waist.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
