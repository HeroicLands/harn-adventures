## Biography

Sir Gowen Karondal is the great-uncle of the current Sheriff of Balimshire, Sir
Eris. The youngest son, Gowen left home at aged 16 and joined the Lady of
Paladins. He fought in the Salt War. In 675, Sir Gowen volunteered to join King
Torastra's forces invading Chybisa in the Treasure War. He commanded the Lady of
Paladins volunteers and earned much renown for his bravery.

After returning to Kaldor in 676, Sir Gowen served the order in various
capacities. His final appointment was command of the Abriel Shield Chapter from
695 – 701, serving under Bishop Ered Curo. Sir Gowen retired at the age of 57.
As a venilana (retired knight), he was given a fine cottage to live in and
looked forward to a quiet life. Sir Gowen and Bishop Parga Indama (Ered's
successor) got along well.

Things changed in 714. The new bishop, Ilor Hadan, and Sir Gowen clashed
frequently and publicly over Ilor's philosophy of the "Reluctant Way," something
Sir Gowen found abhorrent. Despite being 70 years old, he stormed out, vowing
never to set foot in Abriel as long as Ilor was bishop. Much beloved by his
great-nephew, Sir Eris, he could have moved back to clan manor at Hetheron, but
he didn't want to be a bother. Instead, he took up residence a short distance
from Abriel in Olokand, at the Standing Bear Inn. Sir Eris pays his room and
board and ensures Sir Gowen is well supplied with spending money.

Now 75 years old, Sir Gowen is getting a bit senile. He loves to regale visitors
to the common room with tales of the Salt and Treasure Wars. One of the last
surviving Lady of Paladins knights from that era, he is well regarded within the
order and members seek him out when they visit Olokand. Despite being generally
affable, Sir Gowen bears considerable animosity towards Chybisans, especially
those who are also members of the Order of the Lady of Paladins. The innkeeper,
Haselun [FFF 312-2], tries to keep them far away from him.

## Adventure Hooks

Mistaken Identity: One of the PCs is having a drink at the Standing Bear Inn and
minding their own business. Sir Gowen sits down at the table, calls them by a
different name, and starts talking away as if it was 676 at the Siege of Burzyn.
Haselun stands behind Sir Gowen trying to encourage the PC to play along with
the senile old knight.

Wealthy Mark. Sir Eris ensures Sir Gowen wants for nothing. As a result, the
senile old man is flush with silver. Some might take him as an easy mark.
However, the staff is very protective of him. Should any harm actually befall
the elderly knight, the perpetrator would earn the enmity of both the Sheriff of
Balimshire and the Order of the Lady of Paladins; dangerous enemies indeed.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 50 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Axe 43, Handaxe (Axe) 46, Dagger
  54, Polearm 47, Lance (Polearm) 64, Shield 44, Sword 47, Broadsword (Sword) 54.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 19 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 38, Oratory
  55, Unarmed 33, Foraging 29.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Beltpouch, leather as the
  catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm.
- HM3 gear with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Cane.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
