## Biography

Born and raised in Thay, Kym is the son of a fisherman. His father tried to get
him to follow the family profession, but Kym absolutely hates boats. He gets
seasick just looking at one bobbing next to the dock. Kym charted his own course
in life. He got a job working at a local tavern at age 13, and was inducted into
the guild as an apprentice. The job paid very little, but the tips left him with
more silver than his father.

After being promoted to journeyman, Kym decided to go as far away from the ocean
as he could. He followed the Genin Trail up to Kaldor. He moved from inn to inn
as the spirit took him. He came to Olokand to see the great tournament. The
Standing Bear Inn was short of staff. He started as a server and was soon
tending bar. He is a reasonably skilled bartender, but his real talent is
telling entertaining stories. There are some real facts woven in, just enough to
keep it plausible, but they are mostly tall tales.

His boss, Haselun [FFF 312-2], only tolerates Kym's 'blathering on' because he
is also an excellent salesman. He keeps his audience enthralled and spending
silver. Kym builds a story up to a cliffhanger, and then pauses, letting the
suspense build while he encourages everyone to buy another ale. He doesn't
resume the story until his audience has paid up. Charismatic and interesting,
the inn's barkeep always has a half a dozen guests clustered around him
listening to his latest tale. His ghost stories are especially popular.

Kym is also the inn's primary bouncer. When folks get a little too rowdy, he
escorts them out. Those that resist, often find themselves suddenly and
inexplicably thrown to the floor. Kym is a practitioner of the relatively
unknown martial art The Gently Winding Path. He learned it from a Peonian priest
visiting Thay from Trierzon when he was a teen. In his spare time, Kym practices
these unusual techniques at the back of the inn's walled courtyard. He and
Esyra, the cook [FFF 312-4], are good friends and Kym is a regular parishioner
at the Peonian temple.

## Adventure Hooks

Kernel Of Truth. The PCs are travelling through Olokand on mission. While
spending the night at the inn, the barkeep regales them with tales of
Meselyneshire. One story in particular relates directly to their task. For the
right price, Kym can tell them 'what they want to hear.'

That Is Enough. One of the PCs gets into a fight with another patron. Kym enters
the fray unarmed. Inexplicably the PC and his opponent are suddenly both on the
floor and have the wind knocked out of them. Kym directs the other PCs to take
their friend home to sleep it off. The next day, they see the barkeep doing
strange movements in the courtyard. Is he summoning demons?

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 58 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Club 76, Dagger 41.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 63 as
  Discourse; Sales (Rhetoric) 86 as Mercantilism.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 76, Oratory
  88, Unarmed 66, The Gently Winding Path (Unarmed) 92, Weatherlore 37.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Belt, leather
  as the catalogue's Belt, Waist; Beltpouch, leather as the catalogue's Belt
  pouch, leather, sm.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
