## Biography

Haethen was slashed in the face on his very first raid as a young man in Ivinia.
He recovered remarkably well, though his eye had to be removed. This left a
sunken left socket that many find disconcerting. Despite his injury, he became a
skilled fighter. He eventually moved to Orbaal, where rose to become warrior in
the household of Valhakar Zaarli Hulthard in Arone.

Known for his unquestioning obedience and absolute ruthlessness, Haethen was
quickly promoted to the position of huscarl. A clever man, Haethen took great
pains to learn both Jarinese and Hârnic. This combination of personality and
skills resulted in him being made the valhakar's torturer; he excelled in
position and was know for his ability to rapidly extract information from
Jarinese prisoners. As Zaarli has aged, Haethen has risen from huscarl to
confidant and councillor. Haethan's ambition is obvious. Many suspect he has his
(one good) eye on the position of valhakar when the old lord dies.

The valhakar's faith in his huscarl is absolute and he entrusts him with many
delicate tasks. Haethen has proved very capable of controlling the lord's Jarin
subjects. His reputation precedes him and he rarely has to make threats or raise
his voice to get things done. His hollow eyed visage is feared and hated
throughout the region.

Haethen is aware of Jarin resistance group Aenghysa. Through paid informants, he
has learned that the rebels are receiving arms and funding from expatriate Jarin
community in Kaldor. He is planning to travel to Kaldor, using attendance at the
Royal Chelebin Tournament of Chivalry in Olokand as an excuse.

## Adventure Hooks

Friendly Traveller. The PCs encounter an Ivinian with only one eye. He is speaks
Hârnic moderately well and is happy to travel with them; he will fight together
with them if necessary. He seems very interested in Jarin refugees and their
activities.

Outnumbered And Losing. There are noises of combat ahead. The PCs encounter a
single armoured man surrounded by seven lightly armed foes. His horse lies dead
in the track. Seeing the PCs, Haethen calls for help. The attackers are Jarin
who recognised "the torturer of Arone" and set an ambush for him.

I Have A Few Questions. The PCs are travelling in Orbaal. Haethen is in the same
location and notices their activities are suspicious. He will try to make
friends with them first. If that fails, he will move to intimidation. His final
option will be to have his men seize them and use torture to find out what he
wants. If he is somewhere other than Arone, he will do this discretely.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 74 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Axe 65, Battleaxe (Axe) 90,
  Shorkana (Axe) 90, Dagger 68, Shield 61, Round (Shield) 82, Sword 59,
  Broadsword (Sword) 78.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 62 as
  Discourse; Intimidation (Rhetoric) 84 as Command.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 68, Oratory
  26, Unarmed 66, Foraging 59, Torture 82, Weatherlore 45.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Beltpouch, leather as the
  catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Leather Gauntlets as the catalogue's
  Leather Mantle; Shorkana as the catalogue's Handaxe.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
