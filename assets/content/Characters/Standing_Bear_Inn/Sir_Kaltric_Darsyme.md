## Biography

Sir Kaltric is a young man who was knighted just six months ago. His father is
Sir Vulin, Lord of Irians manor, a small, single knight's fee held from the
Sheriff of Thel in Sirendel, Kaldor. As the third and youngest son, Sir Kaltric
has little hope of any inheritance. A neighbor, Sir Marik Kolarney, squired him.
Upon his knighting, he presented him with a fine horse, weapons, armor and a
letter of reference. Sir Kaltric offered his services to Sheriff Ranal Gybsen,
Sheriff of Thel; he had no need of another knight in his quiet shire.

For the last six months, Sir Kaltric has presented himself to the Earls of Osel,
Vemion and Balim, without any luck. He does not consider himself the stuff of
monks, so he has avoided the Order of the Lady of Paladins. His father continues
to give him a modest stipend to keep up the family name. However, his father is
old and his elder brother, the likely heir, has made it clear Sir Kaltric needs
to find some other source of support before his father dies.

The young knight won the local tourney at Esenor Keep earlier in the year. He
deposited the prize money with a Tashal mercantyler in case his brother cuts him
off. The prize also included a fine all black hunting dog he has named Usnarl.
He is trained to drive deer.

Kaltric is a very tall, slim, handsome young man. If he had an inheritance, he
would be an eminently desirable match for a young noble lady. As a penniless
younger son, the parents quickly steer away their daughters away from him. He
has had several dalliances with daughters of guildsmen and commoners. He left
Hutop quickly when the potter's daughter claimed she was pregnant with his
child. He denies it.

## Adventure Hooks

Lone Knight. The PCs encounter a young noble man sitting by himself at one of
the inns in Olokand. He is scheduled to joust the next day, but doesn't even
have a servant to hold his horse and spare lances. He is hoping to do well
enough to attract the attention of a baron or earl. He could use a friend.

Skilled Opponent. A PC is participating in a joust. Their opponent is a very
intense, focused young knight. He is determined to unhorse the PC and constantly
aims for the helm. Sir Kaltric shows them no mercy. He is a sore loser if the PC
wins.

My Daughter! The PCs are hired by a guildsman. His daughter was 'ravished' by
this rapscallion (it was completely consensual). She recently gave birth to a
son. Now no one wants to marry her and the guildsman wants the PCs to get Sir
Kaltric to either pay up or beat him up. This is definitely outside the law.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 72 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Axe 56, Handaxe (Axe) 72, Dagger
  74, Polearm 64, Lance (Polearm) 88, Shield 64, Knight (Shield) 88, Sword 62,
  Broadsword (Sword) 84.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 48 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 66, Oratory
  30, Unarmed 50, Foraging 58.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Knight Shield
  as the catalogue's Knight's Shield; Beltpouch, leather as the catalogue's Belt
  pouch, leather, sm; Long Mail Hauberk as the catalogue's Mail Long Hauberk;
  Quilt Tunic as the catalogue's Quilted Tunic; Leather Leggings as the
  catalogue's Leather Trousers; Leather Gauntlets as the catalogue's Leather
  Mantle; Quilt Cowl as the catalogue's Quilted Cowl.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
