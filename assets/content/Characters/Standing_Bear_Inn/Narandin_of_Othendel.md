## Biography

Narandin has been around horses his whole life. Since childhood, he has
possessed a special gift of empathy for animals, especially horses. Speaking
quietly, he is able to calm and assure them with remarkable ease; few are able
to replicate his success. An expert at veterinary care, Narandin is well
regarded for his skill at healing horses. He has developed a wide repertoire of
herbal cures, including poultices and rubs, which are closely, guarded secrets.
His reputation for saving animals that others consider beyond hope ensures that
he has a steady supply of work.

Originally from the Jedes area, he was the middle son and unlikely to inherit,
so he struck out on his own. A master by age 24, he worked as a bonded master
for nobles, other ostlers and inns before arriving in Olokand in 704 TR. The
Standing Bear Inn's bonded ostler was hopeless; Narandin was between jobs. He
struck a partnership agreement with the innkeeper; he would take over running
the stables as a free master. The deal includes use of the stables and its
accommodations; though, he sometimes has to vacate his quarters and sleep in the
loft during the Royal Chelebin Tournament.

A life long bachelor, Narandin prefers the company of horses to people; they are
more trustworthy and less demanding. Still, as a very successful master
guildsman, with a handsome income, he is the object of interest for several
older widows in the town. He has been known to entertain on occasion, but is
careful to avoid commitment.

Narandin has two assistants: a journeyman, Rhen of Durgal (23) and apprentice,
Coryn of Prost (18). Privately, they complain he is a poor teacher. He refuses
to share the recipes for his secret herbal concoctions, and his techniques for
working with horses depend on a level of empathy and trust that most people
don't have. He refuses to use a riding crop or whip on his horses, but he has
been known to use them on any apprentice or journeyman who dares to do it to
'his' horses.

## Adventure Hooks

Save My Horse! A PC's favourite horse has been injured. Most recommend that the
horse be put down. It is said the Standing Bear Inn's ostler can save a horse in
even the most dire shape…for the right price. Can the PCs afford his prices? If
not, he has a task that needs doing.

Brawl In The Yard. A guildsman comes into the courtyard on broken horse. The
horse collapses and the man begins beating it. Narandin comes charging out of
the stables, and begins beating the guildsman with a walking stick. The
sheriff's men arrive. Narandin is arrested. The PCs are called as witnesses. It
seems an open and shut case. However, Narandin saved the sheriff's favourite
horse and lets him off. The guildsman wants revenge.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 69 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Club 57, Stick (Club) 74, Dagger 76.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 51 as
  Discourse; Hidework 76 as Hideworking.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 72, Oratory
  31, Unarmed 68.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Stick (2ft) as the
  catalogue's Stick; Beltpouch, leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather,
  sm; Belt, leather as the catalogue's Belt, Waist.
- HM3 gear with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Scarf, linen.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
