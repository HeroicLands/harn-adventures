## Biography

Sir Rablar is the brother of Sir Ulaed, the disgraced former baron of Geda in
Chybisa. After his brother lost the family fief by wagering an Orbaalese
mercenary he couldn't lift his horse, Sir Rablar was left without a home or
future. He has spent the last seven years travelling.

Humiliated by his brother's actions, Sir Rablar travels incognito. He wears no
heraldry on his surcoat or shield. He participates in tournaments as a 'black
knight.' He does carry a surcoat bearing his family heraldry, but if defeated,
he prefers to leave quietly rather than put it on. Sir Rablar is not zealous
about people not seeing him with his helmet off, as few would recognize him.
While the shame is his brother's, it is the pity of other knights he despise

Seven years of tournaments have made Sir Rablar a skilled and deadly knight. He
usually finishes well up the rankings and wins often enough to support himself
and his horse. Sir Rablar does not have a squire. He usually hires a local
servant to attend him in tournaments. They are instructed to refer to him as Sir
Black.

Sir Rablar is very familiar with the tournaments in Kaldor, Tharda and Kanday.
He deliberately avoids Chybisa and has not been back there since 713 TR. A
lonely figure, he would very much like to settle down. If he could find someone
willing to take him into their service without prying too deeply into his past,
he could become a trusted and valued retainer.

When visiting Olokand, Sir Rablar prefers to stay at the Standing Bear Inn. He
is aware of Sir Gowen's [FFF 507-8] feelings about Chybisans and has learned to
bite his tongue when the old venilana goes on one of his tirades. Sir Rablar has
made a conscious effort to lose his Chybisan accent and adopt a neutral, vaguely
Thardic one instead. On one occasion, he even slept in Sir Gowen's room, with
the old man unaware he was Chybisan.

## Adventure Hooks

Identity Revealed. Sir Black asks if he can join the PCs for a quiet ale. A
group of Chybisan knights enter the inn. They recognize him, referring to him as
Sir Rablar Tesael, brother to a moron. It soon escalates into an all out brawl.
The PCs are assumed to be his friends and are drawn into the fight.

Who Was That? A PC has drawn Sir Black as their opponent in a joust. He is
honorable and skilled challenger. He fights well. If defeated, he bows and
leaves the tournament rather than revealing his colors.

Knight For Hire. The PCs are looking for extra muscle. Sir Rablar is low on coin
and available. He proves to be a formidable warrior. If the PCs earn his trust,
he will slowly reveal more about himself. He appreciates good comradeship after
travelling alone for so long.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 80 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Axe 64, Handaxe (Axe) 88, Dagger
  76, Polearm 68, Lance (Polearm) 96, Shield 68, Knight (Shield) 96, Sword 68,
  Broadsword (Sword) 96.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Rhetoric 31 as
  Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 86, Oratory
  27, Unarmed 70, Foraging 50.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Knight Shield
  as the catalogue's Knight's Shield; Beltpouch, leather as the catalogue's Belt
  pouch, leather, sm; Long Mail Hauberk as the catalogue's Mail Long Hauberk;
  Long Mail Cowl as the catalogue's Mail Long Cowl; Leather Gauntlets as the
  catalogue's Leather Mantle.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
