## Biography

Caelan is a server, assistant, and general dogsbody at the Standing Bear Inn in
Olokand, Kaldor. He seems to be a well-meaning but disorganized and confused
Jarin from Orbaal with poor grasp of Hârnic and Kaldoric customs. The innkeeper,
Haselun [FFF 312-2], verbally and physically abuses him. Caelan is afraid of his
master's quick temper, yet frequently expresses his appreciation at being given
employment and is proud of what little Hârnic he knows. Everything about this is
a lie.

A salter by trade, Caelan grew up village of Fornin near Sherwyn Keep in Orbaal.
His real clan name is Russe. For generations, his clan has worked the brine
springs outside of the village. Caelan apprenticed to his uncle in Sherwyn and
then was sent to Lorkin to complete his journeyman training under Mirard of
Traegarth [see Lorkin #16]. In Lorkin, he witnessed the full cruelty of the
Ivinian overlords. His master was a thrall to Gard Geldassel. At an underground
Ilviran service, Caelan also learned of the Aenghysa ("Ourselves Alone") Jarin
resistance group.

After completing his journeyman's training, Caelan claimed he was heading to
Arone to catch a ship back to Sherwyn. He left Lorkin and was never seen again.
He had arranged to meet trapper Taumbon of Jamyne [see Lorkin #12] and was led
to Gedan. Recognizing Caelan's fluency in several languages and his literacy,
the Aenghysa leader decided that the young guildsman would be more valuable as
an agent in Kaldor: spying on nobles, raising money from Jarin refugees, and
coordinating weapon smuggling. Caelan was happy to do as he was asked.

The Aenghysa provided Caelan with forged credentials. While the forgeries are
pretty average, Haselun didn't look too closely; he was more interested in the
fact that the Jarin works cheap. He pays Caelan half what he pays the Kaldoric
journeymen and treats him worse. Caelan does a fine job playing the bewildered
foreigner.

## Adventure Hooks

Returning Home. Caelan has assembled a large shipment of weapons, enough swords,
axe heads, spearheads, and arrowheads to load down four mules. He is planning to
personally escort them back to Jara (the Jarinese name for Orbaal) to his
brothers in the resistance. He is looking for trustworthy, but not too nosey,
companions.

Not What He Seems. While visiting the Standing Bear Inn, the PCs have been
served by a friendly, but seemingly clueless Jarin server. Speaking broken
Hârnic, he got their orders wrong, but was very apologetic. One evening, the PCs
see the server acting furtive and then slipping into an alley. They hear him
speaking to another man in clear and understandable Hârnic. If confronted,
Caelan immediately slips back into the dumb Jarin act.

## Conversion notes

This SoHL actor is a first-pass conversion of the HM3 actor of the same name.

- Attributes copy the HM3 scores (Endurance from Stamina, Reasoning from
  Intelligence, Scent from Smell); Perception is derived from Eyesight, Hearing
  and Smell, and Creativity, Empathy and Eloquence are solved from the HM3 skill
  bases of the skills SoHL shares.
- Melee 59 is the mean of the HM3 weapon skills Club 74, Dagger 48, Knife
  (Dagger) 56.
- HM3 skills carried under the SoHL skill nearest to them: Acting 77 as
  Theatrics; Rhetoric 57 as Discourse.
- HM3 skills with no SoHL counterpart, left off the sheet: Condition 60, Oratory
  29, Unarmed 52, Foraging 43, Mining 51.
- Gear carried under its HM3 name on the nearest catalogue item: Coin purse,
  leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather, sm; Frying Pan (Club) as the
  catalogue's Club; Beltpouch, leather as the catalogue's Belt pouch, leather,
  sm.
- The body and movement are SoHL's Basic Folk; skills the HM3 actor does not
  state open at Basic Folk's initial mastery.
