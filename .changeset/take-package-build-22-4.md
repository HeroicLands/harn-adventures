**The site is its homepage and its pages.** The package's home page is published
at `/harnadventures/` and individual adventure documents render as pages at
`/harnadventures/<type>-<shortcode>/`. Autogenerated section landings, tag
pages, and tag indexes are gone — an index between them is a `doc` note that
the author writes. The `/<package>/homepage-root/` redirect is removed.

Taken to stay current with package-build 22.4.0 and hugo-theme 0.7.0.
